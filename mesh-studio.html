What this output means

Each mesh triangle is developed with all three edge lengths preserved. Seam corners are rounded to prevent long spikes at acute vertices. CUT is the seam-allowance boundary; SEAM is the nominal stitch line. Matching E-IDs identify shared edges. This is a faceted approximation, not a compensated pattern for a smooth inflated membrane.

The supplied 800 / 1,600 / 3,200-panel models are geometric approximations of your OBJ. Dense uploaded meshes use a sampled display above 15,000 triangles, while cutting uses all their triangles. DXF contains nominal 2D curves, not CNC-router G-code. Your previous rectangular-frame connectors do not match this source automatically. A CNC knife can follow the textile outlines after machine setup; rigid joints need a separate design.

Pack count covers only the imported mesh panels. It excludes ports, reinforcement patches, actuators, defects, and test coupons. Open edges must be inspected; non-manifold meshes are rejected.
